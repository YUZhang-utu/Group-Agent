# MolPAL (2021)

Graff, Shakhnovich and Coley. Accelerating high-throughput virtual screening through molecular pool-based active learning. Chemical Science. https://doi.org/10.1039/D0SC06805E

Molecular pool-based active learning prioritizes docking calls. Relevant as a competitive budget-matched baseline. Recovery of high docking scores is a computational endpoint, distinct from experimental activity. Compare under identical library, docking engine and total evaluation accounting.
