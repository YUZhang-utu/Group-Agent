# Hierarchical synthon screening (2022)

Synthon-based ligand discovery in virtual libraries of over 11 billion compounds. Nature. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04220-9

Demonstrates hierarchical exploration of a combinatorial chemical space while evaluating a small fraction of complete compounds. Different library assumptions from an already enumerated macrocycle conformer library; useful context, not an automatically compatible direct baseline.
